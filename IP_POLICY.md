We believe that true security comes from transparency, open standards, and rapid innovation—not from locking ideas behind closed doors or spending years in patent offices. Our goal is to build the global open-source standard for predictive security systems.

All architectural concepts, hardware designs (hybrid radio/wired UID sensors, armored MediaTek carrier boards), and predictive diagnostic algorithms (real-time ADC loop impedance trend analysis and high-frequency noise classification) published in this repository constitute **publicly disclosed Prior Art** with verifiable cryptographic timestamps. No third party may claim exclusive patent rights over these open technologies.

### 2. Free Use & Open Ecosystem Guarantee
You are free to build, modify, deploy, and use SPOPS software and hardware designs in accordance with our open-source licenses (**GNU AGPLv3** / **GNU GPLv3**) without paying any royalties, licensing fees, or cloud subscriptions.

### 3. Anti-Troll & Defensive Pledge
Should the project creators or affiliated entities obtain any defensive patents or utility models in the future to protect the ecosystem:
* **Our Promise:** We irrevocably pledge never to assert any patents against any individual, organization, or company for creating, using, selling, or distributing SPOPS-compatible hardware or software, provided they comply with our open-source licenses and keep their derivative works open.
* **Termination Clause:** This pledge automatically terminates for any entity that initiates patent litigation against the SPOPS project, its contributors, or its users, or attempts to enclose SPOPS code into a proprietary, closed-source product.
